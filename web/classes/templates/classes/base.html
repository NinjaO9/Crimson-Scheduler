Crimson Scheduler

Using Crimson Scheduler

Desktop quick guide

Desktop search screenshot

Find a class

Pick a campus and term, then use course details (subject, number, title) to search. Narrow your search using filters.

Desktop add screenshot

Add your section

Select the section you want. If a lab is required, choose both the lecture and lab before adding.

Desktop remove screenshot

Remove a class

Click a class on the calendar to remove it. Use Remove in the misc list for arranged, online, or TBA classes.

Desktop miscellanious actions

Additional actions

Want to change something? Check out the options. Want to export your schedule? Export it as an image OR into your device's calendar! You can also share your calendar with others by sharing your Share Code!

Mobile guide

Mobile search screenshot

Start in Search

Pick a campus and term, then use course details (subject, number, title) to search. Narrow your search using filters.

Mobile add screenshot

Tap Add

Select a section, add it, and the app will jump to your Schedule tab.

Mobile resolve conflicts screenshot

Tap to remove

Tap a calendar block conflict to investigate it, and choose which class to remove from the conflict.

Mobile course interactions screenshot

Investigate courses

Tap a calendar block to investigate it. Here, you can see extra details about the course, or remove it. Non-scheduled courses are available at the bottom of the schedule.

Mobile miscellanious actions screenshot

Additional actions

Want to change something? Check out the options. Want to export your schedule? Export it as an image OR into your device's calendar! You can also share your calendar with others by sharing your Share Code!
