Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 08, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Crimson Scheduler handles information when you use this website. Crimson Scheduler is designed to work without user accounts. The current schedule builder is primarily browser-based: your schedule workspace is kept in your browser rather than uploaded to a Crimson Scheduler account.

Information We Collect

Crimson Scheduler does not ask users to create an account or submit names, student IDs, passwords, or other account information to build a schedule. The browser may store your selected course references, schedule name, display preferences, and theme preference so the schedule can continue working on that browser. Course details are retrieved from the published course-data service as needed.

For security and abuse prevention, the site may process a technical session identifier and, when a session is unavailable, your IP address to enforce request rate limits. This information is used to protect the service from excessive or abusive traffic. Rate-limit records are temporary and are configured to expire after up to 7 days of inactivity.

If you contact the project by email or through GitHub, the information you choose to include in that message will be received through that service.

How We Use Information

Browser-stored schedule and preference information is used to provide the schedule-building features, restore your current schedule on that browser, and display the site according to your selected options. Contact information is used only to respond to the message or issue you sent.

If you choose to create or import a share code, the browser uses it to encode or decode a schedule name and course-section references. The site does not need to receive the code for this feature to work.

Site traffic information from Vercel Web Analytics is used to understand overall site usage.

Cookies, Sessions, and Local Storage

The schedule builder stores its current workspace in your browser's local storage. The workspace contains the active schedule, the term and section references needed to reload it, and the schedule name. Separate local-storage entries keep display preferences such as 24-hour time, hiding weekends, showing instructors, showing section numbers, and theme preference. These values remain on the browser until they are cleared or removed by the browser.

The Django application may set a technical session cookie and a CSRF cookie when you visit or interact with the site. These cookies support security, request handling, and rate limiting; they are not used to create a user account, and the schedule workspace is not stored in them.

Share Codes

Share codes are generated in your browser and include a schedule name plus term and section identifiers. They are not encrypted or a substitute for private storage. Anyone who receives a share code may be able to identify and import the referenced course sections, so do not include personal information in a schedule name or share code. The code is copied through your browser's clipboard or displayed for you to copy; Crimson Scheduler does not maintain a hosted collection of shared schedules.

Analytics and Third-Party Services

Crimson Scheduler uses Vercel Web Analytics for aggregate traffic information such as page views and visitor counts. Vercel Web Analytics is designed not to use cookies for this purpose; its data is handled according to Vercel's policies. The site also requests some libraries from third-party CDNs, including Bootstrap, HTMX, Google Fonts, and jsDelivr or unpkg resources. The browser also requests published course data from the project's GitHub Pages data service. Those providers may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address and user agent, when your browser requests their resources. The schedule builder's image-export library runs in the browser and does not upload your schedule for export.

Data Sharing

Crimson Scheduler does not sell or rent user information and does not share schedule data for advertising. The browser sends course-data requests to the published course-data service and sends ordinary page, session, and rate-limit requests to the application as needed to operate and protect the site. If you contact the project through email or GitHub, your message is handled by that service.

Data Retention

Local schedule and preference data remains in your browser until you clear it, remove it through browser tools, or the browser removes it. Technical session cookies and server-side session data follow the application's and browser's configured expiration and cleanup behavior. The current browser-based schedule workspace is not retained as a server-side user record. Email and GitHub messages may be retained according to the practices of those services and the project maintainer's normal workflow.

Rate-limit records are temporary and generally expire after up to 7 days of inactivity. Vercel Web Analytics and third-party CDN or data-service records are retained according to the respective provider's practices.

Your Choices

You can clear your browser's site data or local storage to remove the locally stored schedule and preference information. You can also avoid putting personal information in schedule names, share codes, GitHub issues, or emails unless it is needed to explain your request. Privacy questions or data-related requests can be sent to the contact address below.

Children's Privacy

Crimson Scheduler is intended for students and other users looking at course schedule information. It is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When it changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to d.montes-vilanova@wsu.edu. For general contact guidance, please view the Contact page.
